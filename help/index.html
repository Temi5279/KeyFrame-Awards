About The Keyframe Awards

The Keyframe Awards is the ultimate digital battleground recognizing visual storytelling, motion design, video editing, and digital creator culture. We celebrate the masterminds crafting iconic moments across Movies, TV, Music Videos, Gaming Edits, Football Highlights, Live Streaming, and Social Media Content.

From viral TikTok motion graphics to cinematic YouTube montages and stadium football edits, our mission is to empower the creators working behind the timelines. Fans nominate, brackets clash, and live votes decide who claims ultimate digital glory.

Help Center & Knowledge Base

How does the voting system work?
Authenticated users receive up to 10 votes per category. You can distribute your votes across multiple creators or stack all 10 on a single nominee to push them ahead in the standings.
How often do my votes refresh?
Vote counts reset according to the current stage of the tournament bracket (e.g., Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Finals). Once a round closes, remaining votes expire and a fresh set of 10 votes is issued for the next round.
Can I buy additional votes or boost a creator?
No. Votes cannot be purchased under any circumstances. All votes are earned equally per user account to guarantee competitive integrity and prevent pay-to-win manipulation.
Are live voting leaderboards updated in real time?
Yes! Leaderboard tallies reflect live votes as they are cast. During the final 24 hours of a championship round, standings may be hidden in "Blackout Mode" to heighten suspense for the live stream reveal.
Can I change or take back my vote once cast?
Votes submitted on the platform are permanent and registered instantly to the database. Make sure you review your picks carefully before confirming!
What video editing software or tools are eligible?
We accept edits crafted in After Effects, Premiere Pro, CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Blender, Alight Motion, Cinema 4D, Final Cut Pro, or custom hybrid pipelines. Tool selection does not restrict eligibility.
Are AI-assisted motion graphic edits eligible?
Edits utilizing AI for masking, background removal, upscaling, or generative backgrounds are permitted, provided the primary edit, pacing, keyframing, and composition were manually directed by the creator.
Is there a maximum or minimum video edit duration?
Short-form submissions (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) must be between 10 seconds and 3 minutes. Long-form video montages or visual essays must not exceed 15 minutes.
How are music copyright claims handled for video editors?
We recognize editor culture relies heavily on remixing popular audio tracks. Submissions embedded from public host platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) fall under fair-use commentary and fan curation rules.
Can anonymous edit accounts or editor handles enter?
Yes! Creator alias names, aesthetic watermark tags, and editor brand identities are completely acceptable as long as ownership can be verified via official social media profiles.
How do Football & Sports edit nominations work?
Football edits (player comps, match edits, club promos, skill montages) are evaluated on sync accuracy, color grading, velocity editing, flow, and narrative impact.
What sports categories exist beyond Football?
While Football/Soccer remains our largest category, we feature brackets for Basketball (NBA edits), Motorsport (F1 edits), Combat Sports (UFC/Boxing edits), and Extreme Sports highlights.
Do match broadcast rights affect Football edit submissions?
Because edits submitted use transformed commentary and visual color overlays, they are categorized as fan art edits. If a copyright holder issues a takedown, the entry will be replaced with an alternative verified mirror link.
Which gaming titles qualify for Gaming Edit of the Year?
All major competitive and casual titles are eligible! Popular games include Valorant, Fortnite, Call of Duty, GTA V / 5M, League of Legends, Apex Legends, Minecraft, and Rocket League.

Which platforms are covered for Streamer Awards?
Creators active on Twitch, Kick, YouTube Live, TikTok Live, and X Spaces are eligible for nominate and vote across all creator categories.
What is Streamer Edit / Clip of the Year?
This category highlights the funniest, most dramatic, or insanely edited highlight clips captured during live streams throughout the calendar year.
Why is logging in mandatory for voting?
Mandatory sign-in blocks auto-voting bot scripts, VPN fraud networks, and proxy attacks, ensuring every creator gets a 100% genuine and verified vote total.
Is my profile and contact data secure?
We use enterprise-grade Firebase Authentication protocols. Your password details are encrypted and never stored in plain text, nor shared with third-party advertisers.
How do I delete my account or remove a nomination?
Visit your Account Dashboard to manage credentials or request data deletion. To request removal of a submitted nomination, reach out through our official contact form.
How do I watch the Keyframe Awards Ceremony live?
The live stream will be broadcasted simultaneously across YouTube and TikTok Live. You can watch directly on our website’s Live Stream Page with integrated live chat!
Will full VOD replays be posted if I miss the live broadcast?
Yes! The full unedited broadcast along with individual category winner highlight videos will be uploaded to our official YouTube channel immediately following the main event.
