About The Keyframe Awards

The Keyframe Awards celebrates the best in digital culture — creators, editors, memes, music, sports, gaming, and streaming. Fans vote, categories clash, and the community decides the winners.

Voting is simple, free, and open to everyone with an account. Each person gets 10 votes per category every day.

Help Center & FAQs

**Summary:** How does voting work?
You need to be logged in to vote. Each day you get 10 votes per category. You can use all 10 on one nominee or spread them across different nominees in that category. Votes reset every day at midnight.
**Summary:** Can I vote in multiple categories?
Yes. You can vote in as many categories as you want. The 10-vote limit is separate for each category.
**Summary:** Can I change my vote after submitting it?
No. Once a vote is cast, it cannot be changed or removed.
**Summary:** Can I buy extra votes?
No. All votes are free and equal. Buying or selling votes is not allowed.
**Summary:** When does voting open and close?
Voting opens on November 4, 2026 and closes on December 20, 2026. The virtual awards event will be held on January 9, 2027.

**Summary:** Can I nominate someone?
Nominations were open earlier. The current stage is voting on the official nominees. You can still support your favourites by voting.
**Summary:** Why do I need an account to vote?
An account helps us prevent bots and multiple voting from the same person, keeping the results fair.
**Summary:** How do I create an account?
Click the Sign Up or Login button at the top of the site. You can register with email or continue with Google.
**Summary:** How do I delete my account?
Go to your Account page or contact us through the Contact Support page and request account deletion.
**Summary:** When is the awards event?
The virtual Keyframe Awards event will take place on January 9, 2027. It will be streamed live on YouTube and TikTok.
**Summary:** Is the event free to watch?
Yes. The entire virtual awards show is free to watch.

Use the Contact Support page for any questions, partnership inquiries, or account issues.
