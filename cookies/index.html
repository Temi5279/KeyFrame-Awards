Cookie Preferences

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how the platform is used.

2. How We Use Cookies

The Keyframe Awards uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential website functionality (login, security, form submissions)
  • Remembering your preferences
  • Understanding how visitors use the site (analytics)
  • Improving performance and user experience

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Required for the website to function properly. These include authentication, security, and basic form features. They cannot be disabled.

ALWAYS ACTIVE

Performance & Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how visitors interact with the website (pages visited, time spent, device type). We use Google Analytics for this purpose. Data is anonymized.

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can enable or disable analytics cookies using the button above. Your choice will be saved in your browser. You can also clear cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Third-Party Cookies

We currently use Google Analytics. If we add advertising networks (such as Adsterra) in the future, additional cookies may be used. You will be able to manage those preferences on this page.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Preferences page from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top will always show the latest version.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us through the Contact Support page.
